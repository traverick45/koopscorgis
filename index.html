Get in Touch Meet the Family

Hi and Welcome!

We’re Ron and Sherri Koopman, and we’re so glad you’re here. Our family proudly raises Pembroke Welsh Corgis in Hermitage, Pennsylvania.

Unlike large kennels, our pups are raised right in the heart of our home. We believe that the first few weeks of a puppy's life are crucial for their development, which is why they grow up surrounded by the sounds and comforts of a loving household.

"We believe bringing home a puppy should be a positive, fun, and memorable experience, and we’re here to support families every step of the way."

– Ron & Sherri Koopman
